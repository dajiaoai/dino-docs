# Contact

If you want to integrate Dino-GSP Open Platform, or get access to community discussion and the latest platform updates, this page is the right place to reach us.

## Community

You are welcome to join the Dino-GSP Open Platform community group for:

- Product updates and platform progress
- Discussions around SDK, REPL, and open capabilities
- Exchanges with the team and ecosystem partners

The community QR code will be added soon.

Before the QR code is published, please email business@dajiaoai.com with the subject line "Open Platform Community Request" to get the current join method.

## Business Inquiries

Typical scenarios include:

- Evaluating an integration with Dino-GSP Open Platform
- Asking about commercial licensing, cooperation models, or service boundaries
- Exploring deeper capability access, joint solutions, or enterprise support

Contact: business@dajiaoai.com

To help us respond faster, please include the following in your email when possible:

- Your team or product name
- The capabilities you want to integrate
- Your expected use case and user scale
- Your preferred communication method
