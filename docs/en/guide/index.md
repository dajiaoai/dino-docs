# Platform Overview

## About Dino-GSP

Dino-GSP is a **geometry sketchpad for the new era of education**.

In traditional teaching, mathematics often appears as static formulas and figures on a page. Dino-GSP is dedicated to transforming abstract mathematical logic into dynamic, interactive, and explorable experiences. We focus not only on the precise rendering of geometric figures, but also on the digital expression of teaching intent — making it easier for teachers to create content and helping students intuitively understand the essence of mathematics.

## Dino-GSP Open Platform

The Open Platform is the standardized output channel for Dino-GSP technology, designed to empower a wide range of **education professionals**.

We believe that great teaching tools should not exist in isolation — they should integrate seamlessly into every learning environment. Through the Open Platform, we translate our deep rendering and AI assistance capabilities into production-grade developer toolkits. This helps curriculum teams and engineering teams rapidly build professional, stable, and dynamic math interaction experiences within their own digital products.

### Our Vision

**Bring mathematics to life.**

We envision a world where mathematics is presented in a vivid, intuitive, and interactive way — whether inside courseware, exercises, or AI tutoring windows — so that every learner can touch the logical beauty of mathematics with their fingertips.

### Core Values

- **Expert performance built for education**: Grounded in rigorous geometric-topological logic, delivering ultra-smooth dynamic interaction that meets teaching needs from basic classrooms to deep inquiry.
- **AI-native for the future**: Built-in structured protocols and programmatic interfaces, perfectly suited for cutting-edge scenarios like AI-powered problem solving and intelligent agent-assisted tutoring.
- **Minimal integration, maximal developer experience**: Standardized toolkits and rich examples dramatically lower the integration barrier, helping education products quickly leap from zero to capability.
